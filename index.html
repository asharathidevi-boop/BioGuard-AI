    About BioGuard AI

    BioGuard AI combines computer vision, image quality verification, persistent IndexedDB learning memory, voice accessibility in English and Tamil, and Adafruit Circuit Playground Express soil moisture telemetry to help growers protect plant health early.

    Two-Stage Vision & Quality Pipeline

    • Stage 0 Clarity Guard: Detects blurry, out-of-focus, or low-contrast captures before classification.
    • Stage 1 Leaf Verification: Distinguishes genuine plant leaves from non-leaf objects, backgrounds, or hands.
    • Stage 2 Health Diagnosis: Classifies verified leaves into Healthy Leaf, Dry Leaf, or Insect Damaged Leaf.
    • Persistent Memory: Manual corrections and scan records persist in IndexedDB across refreshes and restarts.
