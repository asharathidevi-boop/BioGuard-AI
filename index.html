CPX Connection Steps

  1. Connect your Circuit Playground Express and soil moisture probe via USB.
  2. Ensure CPX streams moisture lines (such as Moisture: 42% or raw/percent numbers) at 9600 baud.
  3. Click Connect Sensor and select the CPX serial port in your browser.

About BioGuard AI

BioGuard AI is an AI-powered plant health monitoring application created to help users identify leaf conditions, understand plant care needs, and monitor soil moisture for healthier plant maintenance.

Problem Statement

Early signs of dryness, insect damage, or plant stress can be missed until plant health declines. Combining visual leaf checks with soil moisture monitoring supports timely, informed care.

Our Solution

BioGuard AI validates image clarity and leaf presence before classification, offers category-specific care guidance and bilingual voice explanations, remembers user corrections in IndexedDB, and reads live soil moisture from Circuit Playground Express.

Technologies Used

  • HTML5, CSS3 & Vanilla JavaScript (PWA)
  • Google Teachable Machine & TensorFlow.js
  • IndexedDB
  • Web Serial API & Circuit Playground Express
  • Soil Moisture Sensor
  • Web Speech API (Speech Synthesis & Recognition)

Future Improvements

  • Additional environmental and plant sensors
  • Improved AI classification accuracy and model coverage
  • Enhanced disease symptom identification
  • Longer-term plant health history tracking
  • Individual QR plant profiles
  • Before-and-after leaf image comparison
