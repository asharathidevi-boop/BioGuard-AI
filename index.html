About BioGuard AI

Intelligent Plant Health Monitoring System

System Overview

BioGuard AI is an intelligent plant health monitoring and care system powered by Google Teachable Machine (B-SoEzTPH), tailored plant care messages, disease treatment advice, watering instructions, fertilizer recommendations, reminders with alarms, and real-time Adafruit Circuit Playground Express (CPX) soil moisture and buzzer hardware integration.

Key Capabilities

Detects Healthy Leaf, Dry Leaf, and Insect Damaged Leaf conditions with before-and-after progress tracking, bilingual English and Tamil voice synthesis, soil moisture calibration, and persistent local scan history.
